# One Object — flat-silhouette cover maker

A web app that turns your photo and a caption into a one-object poster:
your photo posterized into a flat 2–3 tone print, standing giant on a flat
mustard / red / teal field, with one huge title. Three layouts:

- **A · Giant silhouette** — the posterized photo, giant, on the empty field.
- **B · Object** — one giant knife with your portrait clipped inside the
  blade, a miniature boulevard at its foot.
- **C · Giant shadow** — your shadow stretched long across the field, the
  small real you standing at its foot.

Pick a sample photo or upload your own, drag to pan, scroll to zoom, tune
the posterize cut, pick the field, type the title and tagline, export a PNG
in 1080 × 1080, 1080 × 1350 or 1920 × 1080. Everything runs offline from
the files under `app/` — no network, no uploads.

## Run

```sh
node serve.ts
# open http://localhost:8080
```
